Pause hosting?

Stop & remove hosting

This disables and deletes the sail-host service. Your host leaves the network — listing withdrawn, no auto-start on boot. Setting up again means re-running the wizard.

Your sats are safe. phoenixd funds and your 12-word recovery phrase stay in ~/.phoenix — removing the service doesn't touch them.

Receive a payment

Paying this invoice credits your wallet. Your first payment of ~25–35k+ sat auto-opens your channel (phoenixd has no manual “open channel” step) — after that you can receive normally.

Waiting for payment… this confirms here automatically when it lands.

Back up your recovery phrase

Anyone with these words controls your funds. Write them on paper, store them offline, and never share, photograph, or save them digitally. SAIL never sees or stores them.

Hidden by default. Click reveal only when no one can see your screen.

Withdraw via Lightning

Paste an external Lightning invoice (BOLT11). phoenixd pays it from your balance — this is irreversible.

Close channel & sweep on-chain

Withdraw your Lightning balance first. Closing sweeps only the on-chain remainder to the address below, closes your channel, and is irreversible — receiving again later needs a new channel.
