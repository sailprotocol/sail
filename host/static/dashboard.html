Stop & remove hosting

This disables and deletes the sail-host service. Your host leaves the network — listing withdrawn, no auto-start on boot. Setting up again means re-running the wizard.

Your sats are safe. phoenixd funds and your 12-word recovery phrase stay in ~/.phoenix — removing the service doesn't touch them.
